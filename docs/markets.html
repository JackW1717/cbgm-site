Operating model

Through regulated local partners

CBGM is not a broker-dealer, investment adviser, arranger, or licensed market operator. Each market is entered with a partner that holds the relevant permissions, so the platform can operate inside existing regulatory perimeters rather than around them.

Local permissions

Regulated partners carry the licences a transaction requires in their own jurisdiction.

Shared infrastructure

One auction, one record and one set of lifecycle tools across every market CBGM operates in.

Cross-border access

An issue in one market can be opened to investors onboarded in another.
