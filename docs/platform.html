01 · The mechanism

The Digital Dutch Auction engine

Real-time price discovery and allocation through competitive bidding. Investors bid the rate they will accept, and the book rather than a bilateral negotiation sets the outcome.

Applied consistently

03 · Capabilities

What the platform does

Onboarding

Guided setup, simple to use

Accounts are configured to each participant's own requirements before they go live. Once set up, the platform is straightforward to use: participants bid, monitor holdings and manage positions from one screen.

Reset tools

Rate resets, calculated on platform

Recurring resets on floating rate structures are calculated and applied by the platform, with the working visible to every party. Standardisation replaces a manual process that varies desk to desk.

Blotter

Trade blotter and portfolio view

A real-time dashboard for institutions to track trade history, monitor holdings and manage risk across asset classes in one place.

Analytics

Live data and market insights

Historical and live market data in one place: pricing, volume trends, market depth, real-time demand, full bid ranges and signs of over-subscription.

Access

Cloud-based, API-ready

Scalable architecture with institutional data security controls, built to integrate into institutional workflows rather than sit alongside them.

Record

Transparent audit trail

Every action on the platform is market-driven and recorded, so decisions can be made and reviewed against evidence rather than recollection.
