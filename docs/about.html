Our company

Why CBGM exists

Outdated approaches in global capital markets have led to funding gaps, reduced liquidity and greater market fragmentation, hindering efficient cross-border capital mobilization globally.

Our approach

We are changing this by providing the tools that stakeholders need to raise and invest capital. CBGM is launching a digital marketplace: a multi-asset platform with transparent Dutch auction pricing that improves market liquidity, expands investment opportunities and modernizes cross-border connectivity between investors and issuers.

CBGM is not a broker-dealer, investment adviser, arranger, or licensed market operator. It works through regulated local partners in each market.
