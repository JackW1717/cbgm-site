The mechanism

The Digital Dutch Auction

Investors act as price makers rather than price takers. Bids compete in a single book, and the clearing price is set by that book rather than negotiated bilaterally.

Clearing price · {{ clearState }}
{{ clearLabel }}
Bid book, sorted by the rate investors will accept Illustrative · $250m offered

The book fills from the lowest rate upward until the issue is covered. The last accepted rate becomes the clearing rate, bids above it are not filled, and every fill prices at the clearing rate.
