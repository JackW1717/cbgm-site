01 · Issuers

Access market-based pricing

Bring an issue to a competitive book, see how the price was reached, and keep the record. Coupon rates on floating rate structures reset through the Digital Dutch Auction rather than by negotiation.

Talk to the team

Deal coordination

CBGM connects the parties a transaction requires: bond counsel, rating agencies, investment banks, multilateral banks and regulated local partners, on one timeline.

Competitive pricing

Bids compete in a single book. The Digital Dutch Auction sets the clearing rate from the bids received, so the rate reflects the demand in the book.

Reset automation

Scheduled coupon resets on floating rate structures run through the Digital Dutch Auction and are applied by the platform. Every participant sees the inputs the new rate was set from.

Cross-border reach

The digital network is designed to open an issue to global participation through regulated partners in each market.

02 · Investors

Act as a price maker

Investors bid the rate they will accept rather than taking a rate that has been set for them. Institutional investors are invited to onboard as subscribers and participate in upcoming Digital Dutch Auctions, free of cost.

Onboard as a subscriber

No subscription or transaction fees on the buy side

Access to auctions, market data and the platform carries no cost to the investor.

Full visibility into the book

Real-time demand, full bid ranges and signs of over-subscription, rather than an indication relayed by a third party.

Portfolio and blotter view

Trade history, holdings and risk across asset classes in one real-time dashboard.

Audit trail at allocation

Every fill carries the basis on which it was made, so allocation can be reviewed rather than assumed.

04 · Liquidity providers

Supports the market

Liquidity facilities sit inside the floating rate structure rather than beside it. Providers see the same book, the same resets and the same record as every other participant.

Talk to the team

Facility inside the structure

An integrated liquidity facility strengthens the credit by providing liquidity in the market when demand and supply for a specific credit do not match.

One workflow, one system

Auctions, resets and secondary activity run on the same system, so a provider works in one place rather than across several.

Reset visibility

Recurring rate resets are calculated on platform, so exposure is priced against a published record.

Market depth data

Live pricing, volume trends and depth across the securities a provider supports.

Instruments

What the platform is built to support

Floating rate issuance, commercial paper and secondary market trading are built and available in the platform today.

VRB

Floating rate products

Long-term borrowing with coupon rates reset through the Digital Dutch Auction rather than by negotiation, supported by an integrated liquidity facility. Recurring resets are calculated and applied on platform.

CP

Commercial paper

Traditional short-dated paper issued on digital infrastructure. Set the terms, allow trading, and run a transparent auction to reach a price quickly.

Secondary

Secondary market trading

A venue where participants post and view interest in securities they hold or seek, alongside a real-time blotter, portfolio view and market depth data. Secondary activity is not priced through the Digital Dutch Auction.

Availability varies by market and is subject to regulated local partners.
